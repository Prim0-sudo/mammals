Mammal Discovery Club - studio word pictures, batch 1

20 subjects in the order specified by redo-batch-1.md. Each initial
generation used its supplied prompt verbatim with lion-studio.png,
hedgehog-studio.png and zebra-studio.png as visual references.

The first squirrel held an extra nut. A follow-up image edit removed it
and placed the front paws on the branch. The full edit prompt is recorded
in prompts.md.

The generator supplied 1254 x 1254 RGBA cutouts. For delivery, each was
centred on a 1024 x 1024 RGBA canvas and scaled to about 85% along its
longest dimension. Export cleared the faintest stray alpha and kept
antialiasing along the silhouette. The cutouts were reviewed at 120 px
with light and dark preview tiles.
