# Selected cards

## mammals

Group card picture for Mammal Discovery Club, ages 4–7, painted in exactly the same storybook naturalist watercolour-and-gouache style as the attached Home menu tiles: fine fur detail, gentle ink edges, warm autumn woodland palette with small oak leaves, ferns and moss accents. One centred composition filling about 85% of a 1024 × 1024 square with real transparent alpha, clear margin, nothing cut off, legible at 120 px. True-to-life animals with no clothes, human poses or cartoon faces; no text.

On one low mossy woodland patch, paint a compact line-up of five distinct land mammals: a tiny wood mouse, a red squirrel, a red fox, a rabbit and a hedgehog. Beyond the patch, paint a narrow blue wave with two distinct portions of a diving whale visible above the water: a smooth rounded hump of blue-grey back on the left and one raised, broad two-lobed whale tail on the right. A short segment of blue water separates the hump and tail, and the whale’s head stays hidden under the water. The whale is a small secondary detail above and behind the land animals. Use all five attached Home menu tiles as painting style references only. Keep the animals anatomically natural, calm, and closed-mouthed. No matte, halo, drop shadow or lettering.
