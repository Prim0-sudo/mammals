Mammal Discovery Club | Batch 5 | Other Animals

All 32 pictures were generated one at a time with the built-in image
generation tool. Each call used the supplied prompt verbatim plus a
reference-use instruction, with two style references attached.
Burrow-studio.png guided the terrain and ice cutouts.

The final pictures are centred 1024 × 1024 RGBA PNGs with the subject
filling about 85% of the square along its longest dimension. Export
cleared the faintest stray alpha while preserving antialiased edges.
Each picture was inspected against dark charcoal at 480 px and at the
120 px game size. The contact sheet shows the delivered files in brief
order at 120 px against the same dark background.

Follow-up changes

- The first newt and seahorse renders showed grey backdrops on dark
  charcoal. The replacements have clear backgrounds and open gaps.
- The salmon was regenerated with closed jaws and fewer dark spots.
- The revised honeybee and ant each show six separate feet.
- The octopus uses an elevated view that shows all eight arm tips.
- Some side views use a slight three-quarter turn to show far feet,
  wings or flippers.

Prompts.md records the initial prompts, both style references per
subject and every follow-up prompt.
