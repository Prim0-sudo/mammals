Bat Night Hunt scenery
======================

Generated one picture at a time with the built-in image tool using the
verbatim prompts in prompts.md. Reviewed the bat flight grid and
vocabulary pond as visual references.

Backdrops (1536 x 1024 RGBA PNG)
  far-trees.png, near-trees.png, pond.png

Objects (1024 x 1024 RGBA PNG)
  moon.png, branch.png, branch-2.png, branch-3.png, moth.png, moth-2.png

The tool rendered at several native sizes. The backdrops were fitted to
the requested canvas and placed at the bottom over transparent padding.
The near trees were trimmed slightly at both sides during fitting to
remove the rounded ends and bring the edge heights closer. The objects
were scaled proportionally and centred on square canvases. The moths
were fitted to approximately 60% of the canvas width.

All nine PNGs were checked on dark navy (#0d1832) for visible matte,
fringe, boxes and shadows. The objects were also checked at 120 px.
The moon has the requested soft glow. Each branch is horizontal and
tapers from its left base. All three backdrop top halves have zero
alpha, and each backdrop was previewed two copies side by side.

The pond water occupies about 9% of the canvas height across most of the
strip. The reeds reach about 30%. The three backdrops are the PNG
originals for the later seam and WebP preparation step.

Bat Night Hunt extras
=====================

Generated beetle.png and roost.png one at a time with the built-in image
tool using the verbatim prompts in prompts.md. Used moth.png and
near-trees.png as style references, respectively. Both are 1024 x 1024
RGBA PNGs. The beetle is centred at 60% of the canvas width, and the
roost is centred horizontally with its roots at the bottom edge.
Checked both on dark navy (#0d1832) at 120 px and at a larger preview
size. The oak's hollow is clearly visible and empty.
