## What is new in these batches

- **Not only mammals.** Batch 5 has birds, reptiles, amphibians, fish, insects and other animals without backbones. Use the same studio photo look for feathers, scales, damp skin and shells.
- **Objects.** Some words are things (binoculars, hay, nest, barn, egg). Their style line says "the real object". Show just the object, cut out cleanly.
- **People.** A few words show children and grown-ups (grown-up, quiet, stay back, gentle, wash hands, human). Show real-looking, ordinary people in plain everyday clothes, calm and friendly, with no logos or text. They should not look like any real, famous person.
- **Look-alikes that must be easy to tell apart:** frog and toad, tortoise and turtle, owl and eagle, newt, lizard and salamander, snake and worm, rabbit and hare, mouse, vole and shrew, stoat and ferret, otter and beaver, leopard, jaguar and snow leopard, reindeer and moose, donkey and pony, camel and llama, wolf and dingo, monkey, baboon and gibbon, kangaroo and wallaby, dolphin and shark, stable and barn, roost and hang.
- **Roar** is the one open-mouthed animal. Keep it powerful, not frightening.
- **Meat-eater** shows a seal holding a whole fish. Not gory, and no blood.
