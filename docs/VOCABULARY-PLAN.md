# Mammals vocabulary expansion plan

**Approved by the owner, 22 November 2026 ("Yes, add them").** Words go into the game batch by batch as their studio-photo pictures are made and checked (`docs/word-art-redo/codex-new-words-brief.md`).

Open questions in section 11 were settled with the plan's own choices: all 145 words; ladybird (British); human stays, in Other mammals; a dolphin is a kind of whale; wildlife hide stays; litter stays; the three safety questions are reworded to mention a grown-up.

This plan takes Mammal Discovery Club (ages about 4–7, British English, teacher-led) from 123 words in 10 groups to 268 words, with two of the groups currently empty. It follows the Insects word plan (approved 16 November 2026) and the Insects benchmark (blueprint v7.0). It also lists the families, "A kind of" links, look-alikes, sorts, about 134 Knowledge & Safety questions, levels, lessons and collections that go with the new words. A word only goes in once its picture exists, because every word must have a checked picture.

## 2. Review of the current groups

- **Homes & Shelters (4) is thin, and branch is not a home.** It has only burrow, den and cave, plus branch, which is part of a tree. *Fix:* move branch to Habitats & Seasons (where Insects keeps it). Add the real named homes of British mammals (warren, sett, holt, drey, lodge, roost, nest, tree hollow) and the homes of pets and farm animals (hutch, kennel, stable, barn).
- **Senses & Survival (5) is mostly everyday actions.** Sleep and groom are things every animal does, not ways of staying safe. *Fix:* keep them, and add real survival ideas: camouflage, curl up, lookout, herd, pack, nocturnal, hibernate. Add listen, roar and howl as the senses and sounds.
- **Food & Feeding (8) is all plants.** Nothing in it covers meat-eaters, hunting or the food of sea mammals. *Fix:* add plant-eater, meat-eater, predator and prey, plus foods tied to animals already in the game (bamboo, krill, nuts, berries, hay) and the eating words graze and gnaw.
- **Mothers & Young (10) names young but not the idea.** There are no words for mother, baby or being born. Deer, pigs and seals have no young word, and nothing shows that two mammals lay eggs. *Fix:* add mother, baby, born, egg, litter, piglet, fawn and pup.
- **Meet the Mammals is missing the children's own wild animals.** Only hedgehog, fox, squirrel and deer are British wildlife. *Fix:* add badger, otter, mole, shrew, vole, hare, stoat, pine marten, beaver, red squirrel and harvest mouse, and fill the three small families (section 4).
- **Concepts that are not things you can point to.** Lungs and backbone are inside the body. Stripes and spots (new) are patterns, and carry is something an animal does, not a way of getting about. *Fix:* keep all five words. Leave lungs, stripes and spots out of the Body part sort, and carry out of the Movement sort (lungs is already out).
- **Near-duplicates.** The verb hide (Senses & Survival) and a watching hut both called "hide" would confuse children. *Fix:* the new word is "wildlife hide", and it is an open question below. Grass/grassland, drink/water and milk/feed are different words with look-alike pictures; they are already kept apart.
- **Kinds are not linked to their examples.** Only polar bear → bear and moose → deer are linked. *Fix:* 41 links (section 5), each kind placed before its examples. For example, a dolphin is a small toothed whale and an orca is the biggest dolphin.
- **The Hoofed family's explanation is not quite right.** Camels and llamas walk on soft pads with two toenails, and a hippopotamus has four toes with hoof-like nails. *Fix:* "It is a hoofed mammal. Most hoofed mammals walk on hard hooves at the tips of their toes."
- **Mammal or not? cannot be played.** Other Animals is empty, and Detective's Silhouette has no whole-animal cut-outs. *Fix:* 32 Other Animals words, all whole animals.
- **Three of the six existing safety questions do not mention a grown-up** (hands, quiet, feed-wild). *Fix:* reword their explanations. "Wash our hands with soap, and ask a grown-up to help." "Stay still and quiet beside a grown-up, and animals stay calm." "Wild animals need their own food; ask a grown-up what is safe."
- **Labels.** No group label needs to change. The ten ids and labels in word-order.js stay as they are.
