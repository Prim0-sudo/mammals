Burrow Dash scenery

The built-in image tool generated each picture separately using the exact
prompts in prompts.md. I reviewed the rabbit-hop grid and the warren
picture as visual style references.

Backdrops, 1536 × 1024 RGBA PNG
  far-hills.png, hedge.png, ground.png

Objects, 1024 × 1024 RGBA PNG
  stone.png, stone-2.png, log.png, burrow.png, cloud.png, cloud-2.png

The tool returned several native canvas sizes. I fitted the backdrops
across the full delivery width and kept them aligned to the bottom. I
reduced the hedge height to keep it close to the brief's 35% target. I
preserved the objects' proportions when fitting them to square canvases
and added margin around the burrow. I cleared isolated near-zero alpha
noise while preserving visible soft edges.

I inspected each saved PNG over dark and light backgrounds for a visible
matte, halo, box or cast shadow. I rerendered the ground with its original
prompt after the first render showed a pale fringe above the grass.
All three backdrop upper halves have zero alpha. I previewed each
backdrop twice side by side. The hedge shows a small change in canopy
height at the join, which is visible in the original before seam work.

All six objects read at 120 px. The stones, log and burrow sit flat
on their bases. The burrow entrance is empty. The two clouds have
different silhouettes, with the second lower and more elongated.
