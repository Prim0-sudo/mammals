Mammal Discovery Club — realistic word-picture style pilot
21 November 2026

24 pictures across eight subjects and three styles. The contact sheet uses
the brief’s subject order and shows studio, wildlife and field guide left
to right. The cutouts were visually checked at 120 px and against light
and dark backgrounds.

The insect macro references were reviewed visually for studio lighting,
texture and cutout framing. The painted mammal samples were checked for
subject context. The new brief guided the whale, kangaroo and burrow compositions.

The studio and field-guide zebra are the closest pair at small sizes. The
stripe pattern dominates both, so their difference is mainly in the even
lighting and reduced coat detail of the field-guide plate.

The generator returned 1254 × 1254 RGBA source images. For delivery, the
cutouts were centered and resampled onto 1024 × 1024 RGBA canvases with
the longest subject dimension at approximately 85%. Export suppressed
faint stray alpha outside the edge, retaining antialiasing on the silhouettes.
