Mammal Discovery Club | Batch 9

All 19 pictures were generated with the built-in image tool. The batch folder
contained no pictures at the start of this run.

The original brief text was used verbatim for each subject. Each call added
a sentence marking the two attached images as style-only references. Each
rerender added a targeted instruction. See prompts.md for every call.

The tool returned 1254 px squares. Each accepted cutout was centred and
resampled to a 1024 × 1024 RGBA PNG, with the main silhouette spanning
about 85% of the canvas along its longest side.

Every delivered cutout was checked on charcoal for visible matte, halo and
grey boxes. The contact sheet shows the full batch at 120 px in brief order.

Rerenders

- wade.png. The first waterline read as too shallow. The replacement puts
  the lake surface higher on the moose's legs, with ripples around them.
- swing.png. The first render had pale haze around the gibbon's fur. The
  replacement has a clearer cutout edge and open space between its arms.
- rainforest.png. The first render copied the kangaroo from the pilot
  reference. The replacement used forest and pond as its style references.
  The same pair was used for the remaining habitat and season pictures.
- summer.png. The first render included a fox. The replacement shows the
  meadow, poppies and daisies in warm light.
