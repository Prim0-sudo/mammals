# Mammal Discovery Club — layered parallax landing, Burrow Bank (23 November 2026, Codex, images only)

Mammal Discovery Club is a children's learning app about mammals for ages 4 to 7. The landing page shows
the title "Mammal Discovery Club", a small pill under it and a round Explore button over a painted scene.
The owner chose concept A, Burrow Bank: `reference-concept-a.png` in this folder. Repaint THAT scene as
separate parallax layers. Keep its camera, horizon, light (sunny, from upper right), palette and
gouache/watercolour storybook look. Do not edit any file except the PNGs listed below.

Save every image in the `layers/` folder here. 9 images. Order: 1 to 9.

## Layout (fractions of the canvas width W and height H)

- Title: centred, y 0.18–0.30 H. Pill: y 0.32–0.36 H. Explore button: round, centred, y 0.41–0.64 H.
- Quiet zone: x 0.22–0.78 W, y 0.12–0.90 H. Calm, even tone; no big shapes, no animals' homes inside it
  except the low phone items below.
- Behind the title (x 0.29–0.71 W, y 0.16–0.38 H) keep it pale and even (light sky / hazy sunlit
  distance) so dark green title text reads clearly.
- Phone strip: x 0.37–0.63 W, full height — all a phone shows. Put two LOW items in it, below the button
  (y 0.74 H down): left of centre a small fourth burrow hole (a mouse/vole hole) in a little grassy hump;
  right of centre the hedgehog's pile of dry oak leaves (empty — no hedgehog). Bring the burrow bank a
  little nearer the middle if it doesn't crowd the quiet zone.

## Shared rules, layers 1–6

- Size: exactly 3344 x 1882 px. All layers line up perfectly when stacked (same camera and positions).
- Overscan: paint 4% extra believable scenery beyond the reference on left and right, 3% top and bottom.
- Hidden backing: every layer below the nearest one is fully painted wherever a nearer layer might shift
  and uncover it. No seams, holes, hard cut lines or white fringes.
- Layers 2–6: PNG with genuine transparent alpha (RGBA), soft natural edges, no halo, no painted
  checkerboard.
- No text, letters, people, and NO ANIMALS OR INSECTS of any kind in any layer (the app adds its own
  animals). No clouds except images 7–9.

1. `sky.png` — opaque. Clear sky only, blue at top easing to soft cream haze at the horizon. No clouds.
   Fill the whole canvas.
2. `distant.png` — the hazy rolling hills and far tree line in the centre, low contrast. Transparent above;
   paint solid soft meadow colour from the tree line to the bottom.
3. `middle.png` — the sunny meadow, the bluebell drifts and blossom bushes along the far edge, the grass
   in front of the bank. Paint solid to the bottom, including the ground BEHIND where the bank sits.
   No burrow bank, no framing trees, no foreground flowers.
4. `tunnels.png` — only the dark insides of the burrows: a soft dark-brown, shadowy oval for each of the
   four holes (three in the bank, the small phone hole), each a bit larger than its opening so no light
   shows round the edge. Everything else transparent.
5. `bank.png` — the grassy earth burrow bank on the left with its three openings (rabbit burrow, fox den,
   badger sett with fresh soil heap) plus the small grassy hump of the fourth hole. The holes themselves
   are CUT OUT to transparency (clean soft earth rim), so an animal placed between layers 4 and 5 appears
   to come up out of the hole. Ferns and bluebells growing on the bank stay on this layer.
6. `foreground.png` — framing only: the two oak trees (trunk left behind the bank, trunk at right edge)
   with their overhanging leafy branches across the top corners, the bottom-edge primroses, daisies,
   clover and grass, the bluebells at right, and the dry-leaf pile at bottom right (empty). Nothing in
   the quiet zone except the leaf pile and the phone items' foreground grass tips.

## Clouds 7–9
