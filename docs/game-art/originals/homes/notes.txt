12 empty-home pictures for Home Before Dark.

Each image was generated individually with the built-in image tool. Every call used
the exact three-paragraph prompt and the warren.png and drey.png style references.
The delivered files are 1024 x 1024 RGBA PNGs. The generated cutouts were resampled
and centred on transparent canvases. Each subject's longest dimension spans
about 85% of the canvas.

The full-size inspection of openings and interiors showed no animals, body
parts, eyes, faces, tracks, droppings, fur or feathers. Inspected the
edges on dark charcoal and reviewed the silhouettes at 120 px.

The lodge diorama shows a visible opening at the waterline. Real
beavers enter lodges through submerged openings.
