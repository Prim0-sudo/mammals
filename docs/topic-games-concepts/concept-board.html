Mammal Games: six concepts

Mammals needs two education games and one arcade game. Each has mechanics of its own, not copies of the Insects games. Pick two from the top row and one from the bottom row. The green "Our pick" cards are my recommendation. Each preview plays its game's core loop with the real word pictures.

Education pick two

Education

Mammal Check-up

Animals visit the vet. Run the three checks, then stamp the card: mammal or not?

You do
Tap a check (fur, breathes air, milk), tap the animal to see the answer, then pick a stamp.
Teaches
What makes a mammal. Whales have a few hairs and breathe air. A platypus lays eggs but still feeds milk.
Levels
Easy: 4 clear visitors, the next check glows. Medium: 6 visitors, one from the sea. Hard: 6 tricky ones (platypus, bat, dolphin, penguin, shark), with no glow.
Stars
By wrong stamps: 3 with none.
Education

Home Before Dark

Dusk is falling. Flip the switches where paths split, so each animal walks into its own home.

You do
Read who is coming, then tap a switch (or Tab to it and press Space) to send them up or down.
Teaches
Home words: warren, sett, drey, holt, lodge, den, roost, nest, tree hollow.
Levels
Easy: 3 homes, one walker at a time. Medium: 5 homes, two walkers. Hard: 7 homes, three walkers and a faster dusk.
Stars
By wrong homes: a lost animal turns back, and that counts as a slip.
Education

Nursery Round-up

Babies have wandered off. Tap a baby, then tap its mother, and she calls it home.

You do
Tap or drag the baby to its mother. A wrong mother says who she is, and the baby stays to try again.
Teaches
Young names: joey, cub, calf, foal, lamb, kid, piglet, fawn, pup. Mothers feed their babies milk.
Levels
Easy: 3 pairs, with names shown. Medium: 5 pairs. Hard: 6 pairs, and you hear the mother's call instead of reading her name.
Stars
By wrong mothers: 3 with none.

Arcade pick one (it becomes the Home banner)

Arcade

Otter Dive

Swim down the river and dive for fish. Otters are mammals, so come up for air before your breath runs out.

You do
Steer up and down to catch fish and dodge snags and litter. Your air fills again at the surface.
Teaches
swim, dive, breathe, whiskers. In muddy water on Hard, a whisker ring shows nearby fish.
Levels
Easy: slow river, long breath. Medium: faster, with snags. Hard: muddy water, shorter breath, and a fishing line to avoid.
Controls
Arrow keys or W and S, mouse, or touch drag.
Arcade

Burrow Dash

The rabbit hops across the meadow eating leaves. When an eagle's shadow passes, dive into a burrow.

You do
Hop over stones, collect leaves, and press Down to hide when the warning comes.
Teaches
predator, prey, hide, burrow, warren, plant-eater.
Levels
Easy: one eagle, long warnings. Medium: an eagle and a fox. Hard: shorter warnings, and the burrows are further apart.
Controls
Space or tap to hop, Down or swipe to hide.
Arcade

Bat Night Hunt

It is too dark to see. Send out an echo and catch the moths that show up, before dawn comes.

You do
Tap or press Space to echo, then fly to the moths it shows before they fade again.
Teaches
nocturnal, listen, bat, fly. Bats find food by sound.
Levels
Easy: slow moths, long echoes. Medium: branches to avoid. Hard: an owl, and short echoes.
Controls
Arrow keys or W A S D, mouse, or touch drag. Space to echo.

Risk: it is a flying game, like Insects' Pollinator Flight.
