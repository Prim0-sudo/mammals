# Mammal Discovery Club — landing peek poses (23 November 2026, Codex, images only)

The landing is a layered Burrow Bank scene (`scene.png` here). Animals peek out of the burrow holes: the
game slides each animal up out of the dark tunnel, swaps a few poses while it looks around, then slides it
back down. You paint the poses only. Save everything in `peeks/`. Edit no other file.

Style: match the painted scene and the rabbit and hedgehog in `reference-concept-a.png` — warm storybook
gouache/watercolour, realistic friendly proportions (not chibi, not cartoon eyes), soft light from the
upper right. Early-years audience: gentle, curious, nothing scary (fox and badger calm, mouths closed).

## Shared rules (every image)

- PNG, exactly 512 x 512, genuine transparent alpha (RGBA), clean soft edges, no halo, no fringe, no
  painted checkerboard. Check on black and on white.
- Head and shoulders only, facing the viewer, centred horizontally. Head top about 60 px from the top.
- The lower body FADES into deep warm-brown shadow towards the bottom and is fully faded/transparent by
  the bottom 60 px, so the animal melts into the dark tunnel. No flat cut line.
- All five poses of one animal line up EXACTLY: same size, same body position, same head position and
  outline; only the pose detail named below changes. They are swapped in place.
- No text, no ground, no grass, no props, no second animal, no shadows on transparency.

## Animals and poses (25 images)

For each animal: `<animal>-ahead.png` (looking at the viewer), `-left.png` (head turned a little to the
viewer's left), `-right.png` (a little to the right), `-blink.png` (same as ahead, eyes closed), and one
special pose named below.

1. Rabbit (European rabbit, brown-grey, the one in the reference) — special `rabbit-ears.png`: one ear
   tipped sideways.
2. Red fox (calm, alert, mouth closed) — special `fox-sniff.png`: nose lifted slightly, sniffing.
3. Badger (Eurasian badger, black-and-white striped face) — special `badger-sniff.png`: nose lifted.
4. Wood mouse (big ears, big dark eyes) — special `mouse-whiskers.png`: whiskers forward, nose twitch.
5. Hedgehog (as in the reference) — this one rises from behind a pile of dry oak leaves, not a hole: same
   rules, but the bottom fades into dry leaf-brown shadow. Special `hedgehog-sniff.png`: snout raised.
