# Mammal Discovery Club — landing concepts (2026-11-23)

Make THREE concept paintings with your image generation tool and save them in this folder as
concept-a-burrow-bank.png, concept-b-watchers-hide.png, concept-c-riverbank.png. Landscape 16:9
(1536x864 or similar). Do not edit any other file.

Shared style (match the sister game "Little Bug Club"): warm, sunny, hand-painted children's
storybook illustration, soft gouache/watercolour texture, rich greens, gentle light, no text, no
letters, no logos, no UI. Composition: open sky/soft area top-centre for a big title; a clear calm
patch in the lower middle for a round Explore button; the hero prop sits to the left third, smaller
details to the right; foreground flowers and grass along the bottom edge. Realistic, friendly
animal proportions (not cartoon-chibi). Early-years audience, nothing scary.

A — Burrow Bank: a sunny woodland edge with a grassy earth bank on the left holding three
entrances — a rabbit burrow, a fox den, a badger sett with fresh soil. A rabbit peeks out of one
hole. Oak trees, bluebells, a hedgehog half hidden in leaves at the bottom right.

B — Watcher's Hide: a small wooden wildlife-watching hide on the left with a viewing slot; on its
ledge a pair of binoculars and an open field notebook. A meadow beyond with two red deer at the
far tree line and a hare in the grass on the right. Afternoon light.

C — Riverbank: a gentle stream curving through the lower left with a beaver lodge of sticks and
mud; an otter on the bank; a deer drinking at the water on the far side; willows and reeds;
stepping stones; dragonflies optional, no fish in focus.
