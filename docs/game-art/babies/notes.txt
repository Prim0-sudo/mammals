11 lone baby pictures for Nursery Round-up. The adult red deer hind is delivered
as ../originals/hind.png and appears on the contact sheet for review.

Each image was generated individually with the built-in image tool. Every call used
the exact three-paragraph prompt and the rabbit.png and hare.png style references.
The delivered files are 1024 x 1024 RGBA PNGs. The generated cutouts were resampled
and centred on transparent canvases. Each subject's longest dimension spans
about 85% of the canvas.

Visually checked that each baby appears alone, with no adult, adult body part
or adult shadow. The hind faces right in side view and has no antlers. Inspected
the edges on dark charcoal and reviewed the silhouettes at 120 px. The piglet
was regenerated with the same prompt to get a cleaner outline.

The seal pup's front flippers have a rounded, paw-like shape.
