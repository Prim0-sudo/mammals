Otter Dive scenery and foods

Generated each image separately with the built-in image tool. prompts.md
records the exact text. I reviewed the otter-swim grid and the holt
vocabulary picture as visual style references.

Backdrops (1536 x 1024 RGBA PNG)
  far-bank.png, near-bank.png, riverbed.png

Objects (1024 x 1024 RGBA PNG)
  weed.png, weed-2.png, snag.png, can.png, bottle.png, bag.png, float.png

Foods (1024 x 1024 RGBA PNG)
  trout.png, eel.png, crayfish.png, frog-swim.png

I fitted the strips to the requested canvas and heights, anchored them at
the bottom, and trimmed the water below the two bank edges. The objects
and foods were centred on square canvases with proportional scaling.
I cleared near-zero alpha noise while preserving visible soft edges.
I rerendered the far bank for a cleaner canopy edge and the snag for a
cleaner base. The rerenders used the original prompts.

I checked every delivered PNG over dark and light backgrounds for visible
matte, halo, box or cast shadow. Each backdrop has a fully transparent top.
I previewed each backdrop two copies side by side and checked that edge
heights and density were similar. The PNGs are ready for later seam work.

I previewed the cutouts at 120 px. The litter is unbranded and has no
visible text. Each food shows the whole animal in right-facing profile.
